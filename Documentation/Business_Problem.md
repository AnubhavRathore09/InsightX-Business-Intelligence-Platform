# 📊 Business Problem Document

# InsightX - Business Intelligence Platform

---

# Business Background

Modern e-commerce companies generate millions of customer transactions every day. While this data contains valuable business insights, it is often scattered across multiple tables such as customers, orders, products, sellers, payments, and reviews.

Without a centralized analytics platform, businesses face challenges in understanding customer behavior, measuring business performance, identifying growth opportunities, and making data-driven decisions.

This project aims to transform raw e-commerce transaction data into actionable business intelligence through SQL, Python, and Tableau.

---

# Problem Statement

Business leaders often struggle to answer critical questions such as:

- Who are the most valuable customers?
- Which products generate the highest revenue?
- Which sellers perform the best?
- Which customer segments should be targeted?
- What payment methods are most preferred?
- Which regions generate the highest sales?
- Which products have poor customer reviews?
- How can customer retention be improved?
- Which marketing strategies are likely to increase revenue?
- Which KPIs should executives monitor daily?

Raw transactional data alone cannot answer these questions effectively.

An integrated Business Intelligence platform is required to generate meaningful insights for business growth.
